ABOUT BEN

Ben Tennyson is a young hero who discovers the Omnitrix, a mysterious alien device that allows him to transform into different alien species.


With great power comes great responsibility. Ben uses the Omnitrix to protect Earth and fight dangerous villains across the universe.
