The Four Houses of Hogwarts

Four houses. Four virtues. One magical world.

“It is our choices that show what we truly are.”

🦁

Gryffindor

Courage • Bravery • Determination

Core Morals

Stand up for what is right, even when you stand alone. Face your fears, defend the vulnerable, and choose courage over comfort.

The House Belief

True bravery isn't the absence of fear; it's acting despite it.

"It takes a great deal of bravery to stand up to our enemies, but just as much to stand up to our friends." — Albus Dumbledore
🐍

Slytherin

Ambition • Cunning • Resourcefulness

Core Morals

Know your worth, think strategically, and pursue your ambitions with purpose. Use your talents wisely and remember that power reveals character.

The House Belief

Ambition gives direction; integrity determines how far you should go.

"It is our choices, Harry, that show what we truly are, far more than our abilities." — Albus Dumbledore
🦅

Ravenclaw

Wisdom • Wit • Learning

Core Morals

Question what you know, welcome new perspectives, and never stop learning. Intelligence shines brightest when guided by curiosity and humility.

The House Belief

Knowledge opens doors, but wisdom teaches us which doors are worth opening.

"Wit beyond measure is man's greatest treasure." — Ravenclaw motto
🦡

Hufflepuff

Loyalty • Patience • Fairness

Core Morals

Treat people with kindness, work honestly, and remain loyal when times are difficult. Value people for who they are, not what they can give you.

The House Belief

Kindness is strength, loyalty is a choice, and everyone deserves a fair chance.

"Those patient Hufflepuffs are true and unafraid of toil." — The Sorting Hat's song

“Help will always be given at Hogwarts to those who ask for it.”
