The Story

Hogwarts had always been full of secrets. But Harry Potter never expected one of them to be Draco Malfoy.


She was everything Harry disliked — arrogant, competitive and seemingly determined to make his life miserable.


Yet behind the perfect Slytherin smile was someone fighting a battle Harry knew nothing about.

"Maybe the most dangerous magic isn't found in a wand. Maybe it's the magic that happens when two enemies finally understand each other."
